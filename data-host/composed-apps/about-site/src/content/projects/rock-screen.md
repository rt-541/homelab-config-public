River rock that came out from under a pool, full of roots, leaves and dirt, going back into 50 lb burlap sacks. The tray is a 24 x 48 in 2x6 frame with 1/2 in hardware cloth on 2x2 slats, hung on two carriage bolts inside a 2x4 stand and resting on hockey pucks so it can be bounced to drop the dirt. Pick the junk, hose the load, then lift the handle: the tray tilts 35 degrees and the rock slides off a flashing lip through a plywood funnel into a sack cuffed on the spout, standing on a hand truck so nothing heavy is lifted off the ground. Three shovels a sack, 45 lb, about 60 sacks a yard. About $190 in lumber and hardware. Design complete, build pending.
