A new 25 by 20 ft composite deck at the sliding door in the bay of a brick ranch, running from the chimney to near the house's east corner, its outer corners cut at 45 degrees, a vented composite skirt below, freestanding on two beams because a ledger cannot hang on brick veneer. The Blackstone griddle and a smoker stand along the east rail, dining by the slider under a cantilever umbrella. One stair drops off the west side onto a 16 ft square of dry-laid buff flagstone holding the outdoor sofa set, with a 14 ft round apron and a 52 in stone fire ring centred on the patio's northwest corner and an 18 in stone seat wall curving around the ring's north half and along the patio's north edge. Between the patio and the house, a stone raised herb bed stands 4 in off the brick, and the AC is screened on its north and east sides with vinyl lattice over mass-loaded vinyl, left open to the west and on top so it still breathes. Five American Pillar arborvitae stand in one row along the back fence on the slider's line, and a French drain along the wet east fence ties into the existing corner drain. Revision A5, laid out from drone photos.
