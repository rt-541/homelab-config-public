The current focus is the overworld: seed-driven procedural generation that ranges from continents to archipelagos, a biome model layered over latitude and elevation climate bands, and hex-grid pathfinding. Saves are real progress rather than bookmarks, and the systems carry a headless test suite (GUT).

Stand-in art while the systems mature. In active development.
