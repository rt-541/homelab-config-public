One box you build, one you buy. The cabinet is pocket-screwed from three sheets of plywood into a 30 in bay sized to a pair of IKEA OXBERG tempered-glass doors, sealed with EPDM bulb weatherstrip and magnetic catches, with 5 lb of indicating silica gel and a USB fan holding it dry. Spools stand on edge in cradle rails, two rows deep, seven per row at a 4 in pitch, 56 on rails plus overflow laid flat in the cubby. Drying happens in a used box dehydrator. About $450 in pine with every price a checked listing. Design complete, build pending.
