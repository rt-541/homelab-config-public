The truck parks in the center bay with its nose over the 6 in landing that runs across the back wall, the potting station and the machine bay take the west wall with the bulk rack on the landing behind them, and the shop takes the east wall. Everything that cuts is on wheels and parks against the wall: a 92 in flip bench with the DWE7491 table saw sunk flush at one end, the DCS785 miter saw on a flip section in the middle and the DW735 planer on a flip section at the other end, each flip turning on a 1 in steel shaft in flange bearings with a riser bench top on its back, and a dust cart with a cyclone on a shop vac. A 48 in birch cabinet on a French cleat holds the cordless tools behind hardened hasps and discus padlocks, pinned to the wall from inside. A 100 A subpanel fed from the basement carries eight circuits, with a 50 A EV circuit roughed in for later. About $2,400 in priced lines, every one a checked listing. Revision B, drawn on the measured garage.
